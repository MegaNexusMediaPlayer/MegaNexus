## 7.0.43 Beta

**Sources**
- 🔎 **Every stream add-on is asked by the ID it takes best**, read from its manifest - nothing per add-on: whoever takes IMDb IDs gets the IMDb ID (the Stremio standard every add-on understands), `tmdb:` goes only to add-ons that take TMDB IDs but not IMDb IDs, and an IMDb title goes by its TMDB ID to those. The other ID is looked up once, inside the searches of the add-ons that need it, so nobody waits for it. Before, a title from AIOMetadata or a TMDB collection went as `tmdb:` to every add-on that listed it: Usenet Streamer takes `tmdb:` but finds no title for it and searched nothing, and the IMDb-only add-ons (Torrentio, Comet, MediaFusion…) were not asked at all next to AIOStreams.
- 🐢 **Slow add-ons are not lost:** Choose streams manually waits up to 60 s for them while the list is already open and growing, and its title says which are still searching ("Searching: Usenet Streamer"). An add-on that answers after the wait (Autoplay waits 2.5 s after the first answer) is kept for 30 minutes, so the next search of the same title or episode - Autoplay too - has its sources at once.
- 📰 **NZB sources play in Kodi:** a source given as `nzbUrl` + `servers` (Usenet Streamer's native mode; Stremio v5's own Usenet player) now plays through MegaNexus's local Usenet player: it reads the NZB, finds the film - a plain video file or a film stored in RAR volumes (RAR4 and RAR5, also with obfuscated names) - and streams it to Kodi from the articles, several connections at once, reading ahead, with seeking. Articles one server lacks come from the next (the add-on's server, then yours); a lost article is a short glitch, not a stop. Compressed, encrypted and 7z releases can not be streamed and say so. Your own Usenet provider: Settings > Connections > Debrid & Usenet > Usenet server (address, login, connections - the port and SSL are found by themselves: 563 or 443 with SSL, else 119; the login is tested), also on the phone page (API keys: username:password@news.provider.com). Help & support has a new topic, "Usenet & NZB sources", with every step. Sources Kodi can not play (an NZB without a server) go to the end of the list and Autoplay never starts them.
- 🏆 **Best stream first, from every source:** within a resolution the bigger file now leads - a release name can be wrong (a "REMUX" of 900 MB), the size cannot; the release type, Dolby Vision / HDR and the audio decide only between files of about the same size. The resolution is read from the add-on's own label first, then from a written-out number (`1080p.UHD.BluRay` is 1080p, not 4K), and a "4K" too small to be one counts as 1080p. Network speed, size limit, cached and CAM rules are unchanged.
- 🏅 **Release groups rated by TRaSH Guides:** the same tiers AIOStreams users import as ranked regexes - Remux, UHD / HD Blu-ray, WEB and anime tiers. A tier-01 group counts as a file about 1.75 x bigger (tier 02: 1.5 x, tier 03: 1.3 x), so it wins over a slightly bigger file but not over one twice its size; a group TRaSH rates low quality counts as a file about 3 x smaller; upscaled, 3D, retagged and obfuscated releases go last. HUB Settings > Playback > Automatic stream choice > Release groups (on). Lists made by `review/make_release_groups.py` (MIT, see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES).

The opening screens keep the HUB's trending picture in the same place, so HUB → MegaNexus → HUB changes nothing but our ring.
- 🚀 **Entering MegaNexus, Home is ready:** the opening screen stays over Home while it fills its first rows (at most 7 seconds, Back skips) - cards no longer jump and vanish under the cursor - and the marker is on Home.
- 🔥 **Trending now on TMDB:** a large picture (35 % larger since the first test build, on the HUB and in the screensaver alike, its logo and lines on the picture's soft left edge) above the tiles - not the whole screen, its edges melting into the background - with the title's logo; it changes every 10 seconds. On by default on the HUB and in the screensaver when a TMDb key is set (Home & appearance / the screensaver studio switch it off); while it shows, the MegaNexus logo leaves the background in all three themes.

**Home**
- 🏷️ **"MegaNexus rows"** is the new name of the catalog-rows layout (Home & appearance > Home layout).
- 🖼️ **Collections keep their own card shape:** posters or landscape as the collection says (Nuvio's tile shape, a Fusion export's image aspect) - no more guessing from the name ("Genres" / "Themes" posters, everything else landscape). Collections imported earlier get their shapes from Nuvio once, and nothing else of them changes; Nuvio's "focus GIF" switch is kept too.
- 🎬 **Default collections are a switch:** Collections > "Cinemeta collections" on or off - off once your own collections are there; turning it on again brings them back as they were.
- 🎞️ **One row at a time, at the bottom:** Up / Down bring the next row into the same place - posters and landscape cards end on the same line, with room below for the focused card's pop (the dark strip at the bottom is gone); the focused title's description has the room above - also inside collections and on Continue Watching. Posters no longer cover the row names when they pop.

**Playback**
- ⏭️ **Next episode at the right moment:** when the credits are not known (TheIntroDB, the media server or the file's chapters), "Next episode" comes 1 min 30 s before the end - the "counts as watched" percentage brought it far too early on long episodes.
- 🖼️ **The next episode as its own picture:** its still (or the show's picture), "NEXT EPISODE" and its number and name, bottom right; "Skip outro" beside it while the credits run.
- ⏯️ **Closed it by mistake?** While the intro or the end plays, the player's buttons (OK) offer "Skip intro / Skip outro" and "Next episode" - opening the player at the end puts the focus on Next episode.
- 📶 **"No limit · fit the network speed" is the default:** with it on, Largest film and Largest episode are "No limit"; choosing a size limit turns the network fit off.
- 🔇 **Mini-player trailers can play muted:** Trailers > "Mini-player trailers without sound" (the Trailer button keeps its sound); Kodi's mute icon and volume bar no longer show for it, nor when the sound comes back.
- 🖥️ **Refresh rate with the video background:** the video background (experimental) gave Kodi's "Adjust display refresh rate" back only when it was still playing - a film that took the player first kept it off, and the background could start again under an opening film (a debrid link still resolving). The hold always goes now, and the background waits until the film really plays.
- 🌫️ **No flash when leaving a film:** with "Adjust display refresh rate" off, the theme's page and our ring cover the screen until the title page or Home is back (with it on, the black curtain as before).
- 🏷️ **Badge sets to choose from:** Stream badges > Badge set - Nexus Elite (the starter set) and five more, Nebula, Quasar, Comet, Stardust and Galaxy: public Nuvio badge pictures (linked, credited to their authors) with MegaNexus rules. Also on the phone.
- 💬 **OpenSubtitles comes on:** an installed OpenSubtitles v3 that was off is switched on once (every language); later your own switch is kept.
- 📶 **"No limit" fits your network:** MegaNexus measures the network's speed once an hour (while nothing plays) and puts first the best link whose bitrate fits 80 % of it - the rest of the home network keeps the other 20 %. Faster links only move down (Automatic stream choice > No limit: fit the network speed).

**Screensaver**
- 🎨 **Widgets in the theme's colours:** clock, weather, calendar and text take the theme's accent and text colours by default, and a new theme brings them back to its colours.
- 🖼️ **Your picture is the wallpaper too:** a screensaver picture or slideshow of your own sets the wallpaper to "same as the screensaver" (also once for existing setups).
- 🌗 **No darkening at all:** no early black, no black curtain, no black cover lifting off the picture, and MegaNexus' own "Dim the screen" is gone. Kodi has one screensaver at a time: with the MegaNexus screensaver off, Screensaver > "With the MegaNexus screensaver off" chooses Kodi's Dim (as before) or nothing.

**Loading**
- 🔵 **Only our ring, no words:** every loading screen, the small pill during a video and the title page show the blue ring until the page is there - no "Loading sources / recommendations / category / streams" lines, no title picture behind the ring, no "Loading…" cards.

**Interface colours**
- 🎨 **Colours that follow your picture:** the accent is the background's leading colour (its vivid parts count most - large dull areas no longer win), and the backgrounds, panels, pills and focus take a clearly visible part of it (Interface colour strength). - 🆕 **Started and left at once:** a title opened and left within a second is in Continue Watching from the start; a saved resume point is never set back.

  ring that fills while catalogs load - no dark frame, no text.
- **Catalogs that do not load are not waited for:** a catalog that failed or
  came back empty used to be fetched again behind the opening screen every
  time (up to 40 s). Now it is retried in the background for 6 hours.
- The image server downloads up to 12 posters at once (was 6).

### Streams and playback
- **TorBox and Premiumize built in (beta):** add your API key (HUB Settings >
  API keys, with links) and their streams appear next to your stream add-ons,
  no add-on needed. TorBox closed its own search, so MegaNexus finds a title's
  torrents by its IMDb id in a public index and asks your service which ones it
  already has. **Cached only** (on by default) lists only those - they play at
  once; off, the others are listed as *Not cached* and choosing one starts the
  download on your account. Add-ons > TorBox & Premiumize.
- **Automatic stream choice:** Playback > *Automatic stream choice* orders
  every source (add-ons, TorBox, Premiumize) and Autoplay starts the first:
  highest resolution (up to 4K / 1080p / 720p), largest film and largest
  episode in GB, Dolby Vision (prefer / no preference / avoid), HDR, cached
  debrid results first, CAM releases last. Rows outside the limits move down,
  never away; your own Plex / Jellyfin copies stay first. Off keeps the
  add-ons' own order.
- **Starting a title:** its logo fills from left to right while the streams
  are searched, as in Stremio.
- **A version the device cannot show is stopped:** e.g. 4K HDR 10-bit without
  a fitting hardware decoder started its sound but never a picture, and the
  hidden player then blocked trailers ("Stop the current video"). MegaNexus
  reads Kodi's log from the start of playback and stops the video only when
  Kodi reports that its decoder cannot show it - never because a slow
  connection takes long.
- **Episodes show their own picture** (16:9 still) instead of the series poster.

### Tracking
- **MDBList as a tracking service:** Accounts & tracking > MDBList (uses your
  MDBList API key). What you watch is scrobbled to MDBList and its watched
  marks show in MegaNexus, next to Trakt and Simkl.

### Sport
- **Sport catalogs of any add-on go to Sport:** AIOMetadata's *⚽ Nogomet •
  Uživo*, *🎾 Tenis*... are recognised by type, id or sport emoji and shown in
  the Sport screen instead of as Home collections (they also ask for a genre,
  which hid all of them). Movie collections such as *Sports dramas* stay on
  Home. Events from such catalogs get streams from your stream add-ons.
- Sport says *No live or upcoming events right now* when your sport catalogs
  are empty (it said no sports add-on was installed).

### Reliability
- **Kodi closes properly again:** a background catalog refresher could wait
  forever, so Kodi stayed in memory after Exit (on Android the next start then
  competed with it). It now ends with MegaNexus or Kodi; a lost loading flag
  expires after 3 minutes; the counter of catalogs being opened is thread-safe
  (when it went wrong, all background work stopped for the session).
- Links in MegaNexus' playback log lose their query part (debrid CDN tokens).
